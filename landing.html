Your library, wherever it lives

Start with local files — zero network. When you want more, point the player at a source you control. Connections happen only when you explicitly configure them.

Local library

Videos from MediaStore. Indexed on-device, sorted, and ready to resume.

USB / external

Pick a folder through Android SAF and play straight from a drive — no broad storage access.

SMB

Browse a Windows/Samba share on your network (jcifs-ng).

DLNA / UPnP

Discover media servers on your LAN automatically (JUPnP).

WebDAV

Mount a WebDAV endpoint over HTTP(S) (okhttp).

FTP / SFTP

Connect to FTP (commons-net) or secure SFTP (JSch) servers.

Plex

Browse your Plex library through its HTTP API.

Subtitles that behave

Load an SRT, VTT or ASS file next to your video, or use embedded tracks. Set the size, move it up or down, and nudge the timing until it lines up. No uploads — parsing and rendering happen on your headset.

01

Load a track

Pick an external subtitle file or use the one embedded in the video.

02

Make it readable

Adjust text size, colors and outline, and choose top or bottom placement.

03

Sync it up

If cues are early or late, apply a delay in half-second steps until it fits.

Local by default. Yours, always.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Local playback sends nothing over the network — your library, recents, resume positions, subtitles and settings stay on the headset. Network sources connect only to servers you configure, and credentials never leave your device. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
